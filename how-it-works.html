How Orin Works

This page is about process only: how One Solution delivers Orin from request to ongoing support.

1

Discovery & Scope

We review your workflow goals, bottlenecks, and first implementation target.

2

Done-for-You Setup

One Solution configures Orin to your business context and required operating lanes.

3

Deployment

Orin is deployed into your existing communication/workflow stack.

4

Ongoing Support

Issue ticketing and iterative refinement keep Orin aligned as operations evolve.

Typical delivery timeline

Week 1

Intake, scope definition, and initial configuration.

Week 2

Workflow alignment, output tuning, and usage guidance.

Week 3+

Refinement support, issue resolution, and optimization.

Support standards

Ticketed requests

Troubleshooting, adjustments, and practical enhancement requests.

Response target

Follow-up typically within 24 hours (often faster when possible).

Escalation

Operational blockers are prioritized for faster handling.
